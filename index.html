Our story began with a single
shelf and a big dream.

In 1984, a small group of passionate residents envisioned a public space where knowledge would be accessible to all. With just 200 donated books and a converted storefront on Elm Street, Greenwood Public Library opened its doors for the very first time. The response was immediate and overwhelming — families, students, retirees, and dreamers all found their way through our doors.

Over the following decades, we grew from that single room into a vibrant three-story facility. Each expansion was driven by our community's needs: a children's wing in 1992, a technology center in 2001, and our beloved café in 2015. Today, we house over 12,000 volumes spanning every subject imaginable.

But numbers only tell part of the story. What truly defines Greenwood are the countless moments that happen within these walls every day — a child discovering their first chapter book, a researcher finding that crucial source, a book club erupting in passionate debate, or a lonely neighbor finding warmth and connection.

As we look to the future, our mission remains the same: to be a place where every person, regardless of their background, can access the resources and community they need to learn, grow, and thrive. We are Greenwood, and we are here for everyone.
